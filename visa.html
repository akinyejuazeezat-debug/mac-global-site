FAQ

Common visa questions

Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?

No agency can guarantee approval — the final decision belongs to the embassy. What we guarantee is a complete, accurate and well-prepared application.

How long does visa processing take?

It varies by country and visa type, from a few working days to several weeks. We give you a realistic timeline during your free consultation.

Do you help if my visa was previously refused?

Yes — we review your previous refusal letter to understand the reason and help strengthen your next application.

Not sure which visa you need?

Send us your destination and travel purpose — we'll tell you exactly what applies to you.
